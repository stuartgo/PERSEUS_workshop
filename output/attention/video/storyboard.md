# Attention Changes the Route

Style: Warm, playful 3D animation with soft lighting, following one small teal robot in a peach-and-cream workshop with coral, gold and turquoise word-beads; no on-screen text, logos or recognizable characters.

## Shot 1
**Visual:** Slowly track beside the teal robot in the softly lit workshop as it pushes a glowing signal through a long, winding chain of colorful word-beads, one bead at a time. Each bead must light before the next can respond; two distant matching beads remain separated by the winding route. The robot's patient expression turns mildly frustrated. Keep every surface free of text and symbols.

**Narration:** Recurrent translation models processed sentences step by step, slowing training and making distant connections harder to learn.

## Shot 2
**Visual:** In the same workshop, gently crane above the teal robot as the winding chain opens into a shared circular worktable. The word-beads exchange several differently colored ribbons of light simultaneously, with brighter ribbons emphasizing stronger connections. Together they assemble a neatly reordered bead sequence. Beside it, the old sequential mechanism is still laboring over an unfinished, mismatched sequence: a playful metaphor for improved translation quality and faster training, not a literal benchmark. No text or logos.

**Narration:** By letting words attend to each other, the Transformer improved translation quality while training faster than earlier models.

## Shot 3
**Visual:** Pull back through the same softly lit peach-and-cream workshop as the smiling teal robot opens the circular worktable outward into several connected workstations. Across them, coral, gold and turquoise beads exchange light and assemble sequences simultaneously. The original winding mechanism rests quietly in the background. End on the robot watching the coordinated activity, making parallel training feel tangible and full of possibility. No text, logos or symbols.

**Narration:** The breakthrough showed that attention alone could power strong translation models, with more parallel training.
